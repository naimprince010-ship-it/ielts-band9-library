# IELTS Band 8+ Masterclass — Curriculum Specification

**Status:** locked curriculum baseline
**Scope:** 24 structured lessons, weekly group clinic, four writing reviews, two individual Speaking mocks
**Product positioning:** advanced IELTS preparation support. It does not guarantee a band score or a test result.

## Learner and test-type policy

The course supports both IELTS Academic and IELTS General Training candidates.

- Listening and Speaking lessons are shared because their test formats are the same across the two test types.
- Reading and Writing lessons must label the test type in every exercise and answer explanation.
- Classes 07 and 08 are separate Task 1 routes: Academic candidates complete Class 07; General Training candidates complete Class 08. Both remain available in the library, but no learner should be told to complete the irrelevant route.
- The course must never describe a technique as a guaranteed Band 8+ result. The four public assessment criteria remain the source of all score-related teaching.

## Publishing standard for every lesson

Every lesson published through the admin workflow must contain the versioned study-material blueprint and pass human review. The material must include:

1. A learner-facing objective and a short explanation of the relevant test task.
2. An accurate core concept with a source or an internally verified question set.
3. A fully worked example with explanation, not only a model answer.
4. Guided practice, self-check answers, and a completion task.
5. A clear distinction between official task requirements and teaching advice.
6. Human checks for accuracy, usefulness, tone, answer keys, and test-type labels before publishing.

Speaking lessons additionally require timed spoken drills and reflection prompts. Reading and Listening lessons require question-type-specific evidence for each answer. Writing lessons require task-response, organisation, vocabulary, and grammar feedback prompts.

## Source basis

- IELTS has Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking components. Listening and Speaking are shared across Academic and General Training; Reading and Writing differ. [IELTS test format](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/what-is-ielts/how-it-works/test-format)
- Speaking has three parts and is assessed through fluency and coherence, lexical resource, grammatical range and accuracy, and pronunciation. [British Council Speaking format](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/what-is-ielts/how-it-works/test-format/speaking)
- Writing Task 2 is weighted twice as much as Task 1; Writing is assessed through task achievement/response, coherence and cohesion, lexical resource, and grammatical range and accuracy. [British Council assessment criteria](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/results/how-is-ielts-assessed)
- Academic Listening has four parts and 40 questions; Academic Reading has three sections and 40 questions; Academic Writing has two tasks. [IELTS Academic format](https://ielts.org/organisations/ielts-for-organisations/test-types/ielts-academic-test/academic-test-format-in-detail)
